# Combining medium-center obstruction tests in A300984

Fix a small-prime assignment from the existing divisor sieve, including the single-coordinate exclusions. Free high-prime coordinates are independent, as in the previous star certificates. Use the same eligible or first-witness prime measure employed in the corresponding shell. Each free medium prime $t$ divides $M=(p+1)/2$ with probability between $1/t$ and $1/(t-1)$.

Work with one sign of the high leaf $r\bmod6$ at a time. The old centers $73<t\le1000$ have leaves above 14 million. The larger centers $1000<t\le10000$ have leaves above 4 million. A leaf paired with an active center forbids membership in A082539, apart from finitely many equality cases. The mixed multiplier-five tests are available only in their prescribed base choices. Previously excluded high coordinates are removed from the neighbor lists.

For a larger center, let $B$ be the set of free primes $73<s\le250$ congruent to the **leaf** $r$ modulo 6. That center's full high-prime risk is available whenever all the primes in $B$ are absent. Indeed, a pre-existing nonlinear divisor $d\le10^9$ containing $r>4\cdot10^6$ must have $d/r<250$. If $d/r$ has a factor $s>73$, it cannot have any further factor, as the smallest remaining nontrivial factor in the admissible $M$ is 5 and $5\cdot79>250$. The only interaction is therefore $d=sr$, for which $2sr-1$ prime requires $s\equiv r\bmod6$. Previously excluded linear tests have already been filtered out. The old centers need no such gate because their leaves exceed the maximum possible original nonlinear-graph vertex. Additional centers using only leaves above 14 million require no gate either.

Write each row's certified failure probability as $h_t$. At level $h$, let $O$ be the free ungated centers with $h_t\ge h$ and let $L$ be the free gated centers with $h_t\ge h$. For an index set $S$, write $P_0(S)$ for the product probability that all its primes are absent. The chance that this level has no available active center equals

$$
Q(h)=P_0(O)-P_0(O\cup B)(1-P_0(L)).
$$

Here $L$ is disjoint from $O\cup B$. If a small-prime choice itself guarantees a high-neighbor test with failure probability at least $h$, put $Q(h)=0$. Such a guaranteed row may share high variables with the other rows of this leaf sign. When the guaranteed row uses only $r>100$ million, every row whose leaves satisfy $r\le100$ million can additionally multiply its survival factor by the guaranteed row's factor, rounding up. On that row's availability event the two sets of high coordinates are disjoint and conditionally independent. Broad rows retaining high variables on both sides of the split are kept at their original factors. Heterogeneous ungated intervals are permitted, as long as their high primes exceed 14 million and their table removes the appropriate previous linear exclusions.

To justify the loss integral, condition first on coordinates outside the high variables of this leaf sign. If the gate holds, the earlier nonlinear event uses none of the coordinates from the high-neighbor lists above 4 million. If the gate fails, the previous event may use coordinates below 14 million. Only ungated rows are available then, and their leaves above 14 million are still independent after conditioning additionally on the lower high coordinates. Given these coordinates one may select a triggered center with largest certified risk. This choice uses only low variables, so its high-neighbor risk is at least the certified value. No independence between different center neighborhoods is required. The other leaf sign has disjoint high variables and may be part of the previous condition.

If the previous surviving probability is $s$, its loss is at least $\int_0^1\max(0,s-Q(h))\,dh$. The expression $s-\int_0^1\max(0,s-Q(h))\,dh$ is nondecreasing in $s$. A known upper bound $U$ may therefore replace $s$. The integral is a finite sum over the ordered row factors. The implementation rounds the two none-active upper products up, the joint none-active lower product down, and every subtracted loss down.

The checker module is [lagneau_joint_star_bound.cjs](lagneau_joint_star_bound.cjs). The [composition driver](lagneau_joint_stars_run.cjs) accepts the same inputs as the earlier layered calculation followed, optionally, by the mandatory negative table on $(10^8,10^9]$, the independent multiple-slope table on $(10^9,10^{10}]$, and extra ungated negative and positive tables with leaves in $(14\,000\,000,100\,000\,000]$. A dash omits an optional table.

The [independent finite-product check](check_a300984_joint_stars.py) enumerates all 4,096 outcomes for each of 32 examples. It allows a center to trigger tests on both signs, allows overlapping leaf sets within each sign, assigns a previous obstruction involving a short high leaf precisely when its gate is closed, and tests with and without an always-active center. It checks the formula for $Q(h)$ directly against enumeration, checks each rounding direction for coordinate probability bounds, then checks the full survival inequality in both orders of applying the signs. Run it with Python 3 using only its standard library.

Codex developed and checked the joint inequality for @dwilfand-sys as part of the analysis of Michel Lagneau's A300984.
