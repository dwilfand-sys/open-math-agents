# Using both signs of the medium-prime test

Condition on a small-prime mask and on its forbidden coordinates being absent. Suppose both signs' rows use only unmixed centers, so that the two lists of medium-center and gate coordinates are disjoint. Let the underlying eligible-prime probability of this condition be at most `weight`, and let the old surviving mass in it be at most `prior`. Both masses are measured in the same units.

For sign $s$, let $H_s$ be the largest of the certified conditional failure risks from its available rows. Each $H_s$ depends only on that sign's medium coordinates. Thus $H_-$ and $H_+$ are independent. Condition further on both signs' medium coordinates. On a sign with a closed gate, also expose the short high coordinates that might occur in an old obstruction. They enter none of that sign's available rows. The two selected high-leaf tests are conditionally independent of one another and of the old event after conditioning on its other coordinates. Consequently the mass surviving both is at most the integral of $(1-H_-)(1-H_+)$ over the old event. In particular it is at most the integral of the largest `prior` units of this product over the entire conditional measure of mass `weight`. This last relaxation allows an arbitrary dependence of the old event on the medium coordinates. Increasing either the old mass bound or the enclosing measure bound can only increase this maximum.

The two marginal distributions come from the per-sign bound for the probability that there is no active test at a given risk. Round risks down to multiples of $10^{-5}$ and their tail probability lower bounds down to multiples of $10^{-6}$. At a given risk take the maximum of all lower tail bounds at that risk or a higher one. Differencing gives a smaller discrete variable in stochastic order for each true risk. Take the independent product of these smaller variables. A monotone quantile coupling on each sign shows that their joint failure probability $H_-+H_+-H_-H_+$ is also stochastically smaller than the true one. Round that joint risk down once more to the risk grid.

Allocate the prior mass to the resulting ascending risk bins, which is the allocation with the smallest total loss. Sum that loss exactly before rounding it down and subtracting it. The implementation can use the smaller of the resulting survivor bound and the earlier sequential survivor bound. The optional routine omits masks with mixed multipliers and those with more than three selected small primes.

Append `quantile` after both extension-file arguments to [the composition driver](lagneau_joint_stars_run.cjs). The computation is in [the independent-product module](lagneau_joint_quantile.cjs). Running `node check_a300984_joint_quantile.cjs` enumerates 32 exact twelve-coordinate models, each with three old-mass bounds, including closed gates, overlapping rows, and an optional mandatory row. Codex developed and checked this refinement for @dwilfand-sys in the analysis of Michel Lagneau's A300984.
