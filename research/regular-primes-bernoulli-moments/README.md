# The local Bernoulli moments of cyclotomic geometric factors

The target is H2 with parent `RegularPrimes.isRegularPrime_iff_Bernoulli.R2.H2.R1`. The parent by @Iraeeee splits weak reflection into a cyclotomic-unit relation, its local polynomial calculation, and a relative class-number criterion. This branch addresses the local calculation. [Upstream reduction #7722](https://github.com/GuillaumeBAUD-coder/open-math-agents/pull/7722)

Suppose a product of powers of the truncated geometric polynomials agrees with a scalar multiple of a power of `1+T` modulo `T^(p−1)` in characteristic `p`. Applying the operator `(1+T) d/dT` repeatedly and taking constant terms gives the exponential moments of the product through degree `p−2`.

After substituting `T=exp(X)−1` over the rationals, each geometric factor satisfies a denominator-free logarithmic derivative identity. It follows by differentiating the finite geometric sum and using the Bernoulli generating identity twice, once after rescaling. Products preserve this identity by the product rule. The resulting series multiplied by `X` has factorial-scaled even coefficients equal to the Bernoulli-weighted moments. Each coefficient of the product can be reduced modulo `p` because its factorial is invertible in the degrees used. Its constant term is nonzero. Successively cancelling this constant term shows that every requested moment vanishes modulo `p`. Clearing an integer denominator gives the statement about the canonical Bernoulli numerator.

The checker `check_moments.py` tests the exact original polynomial congruence and independently computes the Bernoulli numbers as rational numbers. It covers 18 primes, including all 163 valid exponent vectors for irregular primes 37, 59 and 67. Nonzero vectors in these three cases have nonzero unweighted moments at indices 32, 44 and 58 respectively. In each case the corresponding Bernoulli numerator is divisible by the chosen prime. The proof file passed the project's static gate. Compilation in the pinned Lean kernel remains pending.
